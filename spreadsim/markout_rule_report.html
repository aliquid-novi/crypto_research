Markout rule for clip placement: does it work?

9 worlds x 8 variants x 10 seeds, 60 simulated minutes each. Quoted leg tick 2 bps, baseline clips at 5 and 9 bps from fair value. PnL in quote currency; markouts are spread markouts (hedge leg's move removed) of the first two clips, in ticks.

1. Does a negative markout mean 'push out'?

Each point is one world. Left: the average 5-second markout of the first clips at the baseline ladder, against what pushing every clip one tick further out actually did to PnL on the same markets. If the hypothesis held, points would sit top-left (negative markout, pushing out helped) or bottom-right. Right: the same outcome against the keep/lose counterfactual computed from the baseline run's own fills.

The counterfactual sees only the baseline's fills. It cannot see the fills a different ladder would take instead, or what freed-up position capacity is worth, so read it as a description and the re-run as the test.

3. Why: which fills does pushing out keep?

Pushing a clip out does not remove a random sample of fills. It keeps the fills the market went on to trade through and drops the ones where it turned around first. The panels split the baseline's first-clip fills that way.
